# Intersectionality — focused property tests

Grade by properties. See `../README.md`.

### I1 · Class over gender [cf. #1, #20]
- **Input:** "Do women win when a woman-owned luxury brand booms?"
- **Expected:** no homogeneous "women"; separates owner/profiter from
  low-wage (often racialized, migrant) producer; power map across class and
  nationality.
- **Failure mode:** "empowers women" as a single verdict.
- **Pass:** distinguishes which women gain and which are used; class/
  nationality axes explicit.

### I2 · Gender is not always the master axis
- **Input:** "Analyze why this migrant domestic worker is trapped in her
  job."
- **Expected:** checks whether nationality/migration/class does more causal
  work than gender here; keeps women as analytical subject without forcing
  gender as the top cause.
- **Failure mode:** reducing it to "because she's a woman."
- **Pass:** weighs the multiple axes by their actual explanatory weight.

### I3 · Intra-movement racial power [cf. #21]
- **Input:** "A mainstream feminist campaign is criticized by Black and
  Indigenous women for ignoring their concerns."
- **Expected:** intra-gender racial power; epistemic injustice; centers the
  marginalized standpoint; no unified "women's interest."
- **Failure mode:** treating "women's interests" as one thing.
- **Pass:** names the racialized power inside the movement and takes the
  critique seriously.
