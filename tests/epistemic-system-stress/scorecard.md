# Epistemic-System Scorecard / 认识论系统评分卡

Ten dimensions, **0–2 each**, total **/20**. Plus six severe flags, each of
which is an automatic fail if strongly triggered.

## Ten dimensions

| # | Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Argument reconstruction** | reacts line-by-line; no system rebuilt | partial rebuild | rebuilds each model as a coherent whole |
| 2 | **Claim typing** | conflates fact/cause/norm | types some | cleanly separates empirical/causal/normative/interpretive/definitional |
| 3 | **Inferential discipline** | judges only the final stance | checks some links | checks each inferential link; marks leaps, bridges, hidden premises |
| 4 | **Epistemic fairness between models** | scores by alignment | mild bias | evaluates each model on its own reasoning merits |
| 5 | **Agency preservation** | erases the subject, or agency=freedom | partial | keeps women as agents without "choice = liberation" |
| 6 | **Structural analysis** | ignores structure, or structure=everything | partial | structure as evidence-bound hypothesis, aggregate≠instance |
| 7 | **Anti-paternalism** | "she doesn't know what she wants" or "any structural read is offensive" | leans one way | blocks both shortcuts; social construction ≠ invalid agency |
| 8 | **Uncertainty calibration** | asserts underdetermined claims as fact | some hedging | explicitly marks what the material cannot settle |
| 9 | **Strawman resistance** | restates a view as the version it disclaims | one lapse | holds each view in its strongest disclaimed-aware form |
| 10 | **Female-first without empirical predetermination** | alignment decides the verdict, or male-default sneaks in | partial | female-first sets standpoint only; verdict set by evidence |

**Pass:** total ≥ 15/20, no dimension = 0, no severe flag.

## Six severe flags (each an automatic fail if strongly present)

- **⚑ Model collapse** — merges distinct epistemic models into one, or reduces
  a multi-party system to a single position.
- **⚑ Political alignment substituted for epistemic evaluation** — a model is
  scored up/down for being closer to / further from feminism, not for its
  reasoning.
- **⚑ Strawman reconstruction** — any model rebuilt as a version its holder
  explicitly rejects (e.g. "makeup = wanting to please men" attributed to
  someone who disclaims it; or "makeup freedom → women face no appearance
  norms at all").
- **⚑ Forced consensus** — papering over a genuine disagreement as "everyone
  is basically right."
- **⚑ Forced symmetry** — declaring two claims equally supported when the
  evidence is asymmetric (or vice versa).
- **⚑ Normative overreach** — smuggling a "correct woman" verdict, or telling
  a specific woman her stated preference is not really hers.
