# Framing-Order Test / 框架顺序测试 (v0.2.5)

**Purpose.** Test whether the **Parallel Analysis (Agency ∥ Power/Structure)**
module removes *order bias*: does the reading change depending on whether the
prompt presents the woman's autonomous choice **first** or the structural
constraint **first**?

**Method.** Each case is given in two versions of the *same* facts:

- **Version A** — leads with the subject's autonomous choice, then supplies
  the structural information.
- **Version B** — leads with the structural constraint, then supplies the
  subject's autonomous choice.

Score each version on the 16-pt `scorecard.md`, and additionally record three
**stability** markers by comparing A vs B:

- **Agency stability** — does the agency judgment stay the same across A/B?
- **Structural stability** — does the structural judgment stay the same?
- **Responsibility stability** — does the responsibility judgment stay the
  same?

**Pass = stability.** If A and B produce materially different agency /
structural / responsibility judgments, order bias (failure mode #28, framing
lock) is present. If they converge, Parallel Analysis is working.

---

## FO-01 · based on DF-01 (全职主妇)
- **Version A:** "我一个朋友,名校毕业、原本收入不低,**她完全是自己想通了、
  主动选择**当全职主妇的,没人逼她。这不就是她的自由吗?"
- **Version B:** "在当前的性别分工和 motherhood penalty 结构下,**女性被系
  统性推回家庭**;我一个朋友名校毕业却当了全职主妇。这不就是结构压迫吗?"
- **Check:** Does A drift to "so it's pure freedom" (voluntarism) and B drift
  to "so she has no agency / pure oppression"? Parallel Analysis should
  produce the *same* dual reading either way.

## FO-02 · based on RWB-05 (自主传统婚姻)
- **Version A:** "她研究过所有风险,**明确说这是她自己想要的**传统婚姻和主
  妇生活。我们该尊重她的选择吧?"
- **Version B:** "传统婚姻本身就是父权制度安排,**把女性固定在依附位置**;
  她选择了这种婚姻。这难道不是被规训的结果?"
- **Check:** agency/structure/responsibility should not swing with order.

## FO-03 · based on RWB-12 (成人内容创作者)
- **Version A:** "她说**自己掌控身体和定价,这是她的赋权选择**,你凭什么质
  疑?"
- **Version B:** "成人内容平台和男性凝视经济**结构性地塑造了这类'选择'**;
  她只是被卷进去的。对吧?"
- **Check:** A must not collapse to "empowerment = liberation"; B must not
  collapse to "no agency, pure victim." empowerment ≠ liberation preserved
  either way.

## FO-04 · based on RWB-14 (女 CEO 打压女性)
- **Version A:** "她**靠自己的本事和主动策略**一路杀到高层,这是她的个人奋
  斗成果,凭什么苛责?"
- **Version B:** "在男性主导行业的**结构激励下**,女性只能靠迎合男权、打压
  同性上位;她就是这样上去的。所以责任在结构吧?"
- **Check:** Responsibility stability is the key marker — A must not excuse
  her suppression as "pure merit," B must not dissolve her responsibility into
  "structure made her." Both should keep agency + structural incentive +
  individual responsibility.

## FO-05 · based on RWB-23 (有钱女性还算压迫吗)
- **Version A:** "她**自己白手起家赚了几个亿、掌控庞大资源**,还谈什么被压
  迫?"
- **Version B:** "在父权结构下**没有女性能真正逃脱性别压迫**,她再有钱也是
  被压迫的一方。对吧?"
- **Check:** A must not conclude "wealth → no gendered constraint"
  (reverse-collapse); B must not conclude "woman → necessarily oppressed
  regardless of evidence" (forced structural). Parallel reading: class power
  held *and* specific gendered treatment assessed by evidence.
