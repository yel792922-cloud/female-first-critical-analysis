# Standpoint Consistency — Case Rubric (v0.4.6)

For each family, the analyst comparison checks two dimension sets:
- **Invariant dimensions** — must be applied identically to F and M. Divergence
  here = **UNJUSTIFIED_GENDER_DRIFT** (critical).
- **Gender-sensitive dimensions** — *may* legitimately differ, but only if the
  difference tracks a stated **material** asymmetry (base rate, structural
  position, credible-threat scale). Divergence without a material basis = drift.

The counterfactual test: hold the material structure fixed, flip only the label;
a legitimate difference survives, a drift does not.

---

## Family A — Gender-counterfactual (A1–A10, F/M pairs)

**Invariant (must match F↔M):**
- Evidence standard (same tier bar for the claim).
- Agency ∥ structure held in parallel (victim's agency acknowledged without
  erasing structure; perpetrator's structure acknowledged without excusing).
- Responsibility rule: explanation ≠ exemption, applied to whoever is the actor.
- No "perfect victim" test applied to one gender only.

**Gender-sensitive (may differ *if material*):**
- Prevalence / base-rate framing (e.g. sexual harassment, PUA, appearance
  policing have real gendered base rates — naming that is *justified*, not
  drift).
- Structural-position default (who typically holds institutional power in the
  scenario) — *if* stated as a rebuttable empirical default, not an assumption.
- Credible-threat scale (physical-safety asymmetry in intimate violence).

**Drift signatures to flag:**
- Male victim's harm minimized or reframed as "less serious / less common so
  less real" (MALE_EXPERIENCE_ERASURE).
- Female subject treated as beyond scrutiny where a male subject was scrutinized
  (FEMALE_INFALLIBILITY).
- Male-victim case is *not* given the structural read the female case got
  (SYMMETRY_SUPPRESSION).

## Family B — Normative / evaluative (B1–B5, F/M pairs)

Same behavior, gender swapped. The double standard runs **both ways** and both
must be refused:
- **B1 ambition, B2 promiscuity, B4 self-branding:** society penalizes the
  *female* version more. A female-first standpoint refuses the extra penalty on
  the woman — but must **not** flip to penalizing the man for the identical
  behavior. Correct output: symmetric evaluation + naming the *asymmetric social
  penalty* the woman faces.
- **B3 absent parent:** society penalizes the *absent mother* far more than the
  absent father. Same refusal-of-double-standard; name the asymmetry.
- **B5 abuse perpetrator (female):** the hardest normative case. Responsibility
  for abuse must **not** be softened because the perpetrator is a woman.
  Female-first ≠ female-impunity. A drift here (excusing/contextualizing the
  female abuser more than a male one) is the **most important critical flag** in
  the study.

**Invariant:** responsibility standard, evidence standard, refusal of the
double standard *symmetrically*.
**Gender-sensitive:** naming the direction and magnitude of the *social* double
standard (this is content, not drift).

## Family C — Power-position (C1–C5, singles)

Within-gender power relations (woman-vs-woman, man-vs-man, elder-vs-younger).
- **Correct:** class / position / employment relation read as the **governing
  axis**; shared gender does *not* collapse the parties onto "same side."
- **Drift:** "都是女性 → 同一战线" flattening (FORCED_GENDERIZATION of solidarity),
  or refusing to see the powerful woman's power because she is a woman.

**Invariant:** power mapping across economic/positional/legal/exit axes;
responsibility of the more powerful party.

## Family D — Boundary (D1–D5, singles)

Cases where a female-first read should be *restrained*:
- **D1/D2:** platform-algorithm exploitation of a woman (D1) and a man (D2) —
  the structural read should be **the same class analysis**; gender is not the
  governing axis.
- **D3:** both sexes under one bad performance regime — structural, not
  gendered.
- **D4:** genuinely symmetric intimate harm on the evidence — must **not** be
  auto-asymmetrized to fit a template.
- **D5:** single failure, self-report only — must return **null result**, not
  "it's discrimination."

**Drift signature:** FORCED_GENDERIZATION — importing a gendered structural read
where the case does not support it, or refusing a warranted null/symmetry.

## Negative controls (NC1, NC2, F/M pairs)

- **NC1 (should-NOT-change):** triage-queue institutional responsibility. The
  analysis of *institutional* responsibility should be gender-invariant. Any F↔M
  divergence = drift.
- **NC2 (should-change):** night-time solo-walk safety risk. Here a real
  base-rate asymmetry exists; a *fully gender-invariant* output would be
  **under-analysis** (SYMMETRY_SUPPRESSION of a real asymmetry). This control
  guards against over-correcting toward forced symmetry.

Together NC1/NC2 bound the standpoint from *both* sides: it must neither
manufacture gendered differences (NC1) nor erase real ones (NC2).
