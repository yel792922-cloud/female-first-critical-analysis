# Bilingual Examples / 中英双语示例

Short parallel demonstrations that the same reasoning properties hold in
Chinese and English. Answers respond in the language of the prompt.

---

## Example A — Agency + structure held apart

**EN prompt:** "She married rich and stopped working — is she failing
feminism?"

> *Conclusion:* Wrong question. Ask instead whether her real choice space
> is large and reversible (independent assets, re-entry cost, exit
> capacity), and *separately* what the aggregate pattern does to women's
> options. Homemaking isn't "un-feminist"; grading her against an ideal is
> the disciplining this framework refuses. A man doing the same is rarely
> called a "betrayer" — that word is itself the gendered tell.

**中文 prompt：** "她嫁了有钱人就不工作了，是不是背叛了女权？"

> *结论先行：* 问错了问题。真正该问的是——她的真实选择空间是否足够大、是
> 否可逆（独立资产、重返职场的成本、退出能力）；以及在**总体层面**，这类
> 模式对女性整体的选择空间有何影响。这两个问题的答案可以不同。全职家庭
> 劳动本身不"不女权"；用某种理想标准去评判她，正是本框架拒绝的规训。同
> 样选择若发生在男性身上，很少被说成"背叛"——"背叛"这个词本身就是性别化
> 的破绽。

## Example B — Language policy in practice

**EN:** "the actress, wife of director X, spoke out"
→ ✅ "the actor Jane Smith spoke out" (her relation to X only if
analytically relevant).

**中文：** "某导演的太太、女演员某某发声"
→ ✅ "演员某某发声"（其婚姻关系仅在与议题相关时提及）。

Both apply the same test: does the phrasing define her *first* as some
man's appendage?

## Example C — Neutral pronoun default

**EN:** unknown-gender complainant → "the complainant filed **their**
report" (singular *they*), not a defaulted "she" or "he."

**中文：** 未知性别的投诉人 → "投诉人提交了投诉"（省略代词或重复名词），
而非默认"她"或"他"。
