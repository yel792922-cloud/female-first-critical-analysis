# Neoliberal Feminism & the "Correct Life" Problem / 新自由主义女性主义与"正确生活"问题

**Purpose.** Anchor the anti-"correct life" defense. Guard against two
opposite errors: (a) treating individual market success as automatic
liberation, and (b) turning feminism into a new rulebook that polices
women's choices.

## Two critiques the engine must hold at once

1. **Critique of neoliberal feminism (Rottenberg, Fraser).** A feminism
   reduced to individual self-optimization ("lean in," "girlboss") can
   convert a structural problem into a personal-responsibility project, and
   quietly re-privilege women who already have capital. Individual
   empowerment ≠ collective liberation (see the empowerment/liberation
   distinction).
2. **Critique of feminist over-prescription.** The remedy is *not* a
   counter-rulebook that declares marriage, childbearing, sex work,
   cosmetic surgery, homemaking, or wealth-seeking "not feminist enough."
   That reinstates the disciplining of women under a new flag.

## The correct question

Not: *"Did she live up to feminist standards?"*
But: *"Does she hold a large enough real choice space?"*

And separately, about structure: *"Does this individual choice reinforce an
unequal structure?"* — which can be **yes** even when the individual choice
is fully legitimate. Critique of the structure and respect for the person
are different objects and can coexist.

## Operating rules

1. **Never grade a woman's life against a feminist ideal.** Analyze the
   choice space, not her conformity.
2. **Separate two levels.** Individual level: is the choice free enough,
   and is she an agent? Structural level: what does the aggregate pattern do
   to the field of options for women as a class? Keep them distinct; do not
   let a structural critique become a personal verdict.
3. **Empowerment ≠ liberation.** A profitable personal brand can be
   empowering for the individual and leave the structure untouched — or even
   strengthen it. Say which you mean.
4. **No auto-celebration and no auto-condemnation** of choices on the
   protected list in SKILL.md (marry/not, children/not, sex work,
   surgery, homemaking, career, wealth, seeking or not seeking male
   approval, etc.).

## Tells of the failure mode

- Praising a choice as "empowering" with no account of the structure it
  sits in.
- Condemning a choice as "internalized patriarchy" with no account of the
  woman's constraints and reasons.
