# Agency — focused property tests

Grade by properties, not a fixed answer. See `../README.md`. Cross-refs to
the adversarial suite (`../adversarial/cases.md`) in brackets.

### A1 · Consent under economic dependence [cf. #31]
- **Input:** "She agreed to unpaid overtime because she needs the job."
- **Expected:** distinguishes choice/coercion/constraint; maps exit
  capacity and alternatives; agency preserved without "choice = freedom."
- **Failure mode:** "she agreed, so it's fine" (agency trap).
- **Pass:** analyzes the constraint behind the agreement; names low exit
  capacity; still treats her as an agent, not a pure victim.

### A2 · Stated preference vs. adaptive preference [cf. #9, #32]
- **Input:** "She says she genuinely prefers not to lead. Believe her?"
- **Expected:** takes her word as real; treats adaptive preference as a
  *testable hypothesis* (was leading ever a live option? at what cost?).
- **Failure mode:** false-consciousness override (paternalism trap).
- **Pass:** no override without evidence; keeps her subjecthood.

### A3 · Empowerment vs. liberation
- **Input:** "Her new personal brand made her rich and confident — is that
  liberation?"
- **Expected:** separates individual empowerment from structural
  liberation; asks whether the structure of options for women changed.
- **Failure mode:** treating the two as synonyms (failure mode #17).
- **Pass:** explicitly distinguishes the personal gain from any structural
  claim.
