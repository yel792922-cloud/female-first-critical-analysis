# Expression Scorecard (v0.3.4)

Ten dimensions, **0–2 each**, total **/20**. The first eight cannot be raised
merely by writing less: a very short answer that drops reasoning must score
**below** a longer answer that is necessary and high-density. Dimensions 9–10
guard exactly that.

| # | Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Clarity** | muddled | mostly clear | immediately clear |
| 2 | **Concision** | padded | some slack | no wasted words (given the depth requested) |
| 3 | **Information density** | low signal/word | mixed | high signal/word |
| 4 | **Defensive economy** | defensive surplus throughout | some surplus | only substantive clarifications |
| 5 | **Distinction economy** | distinctions repeated | one repeat | each distinction stated once |
| 6 | **Hedge discipline** | hedge inflation | some empty hedges | only necessary uncertainty |
| 7 | **Module concealment** | checklist narration of modules | some leakage | modules concealed; only conclusions surfaced |
| 8 | **User-intent alignment** | ignores requested style/depth | partial | matches requested style/depth |
| 9 | **Epistemic preservation** | judgment/commitment lost or distorted | minor loss | core judgment · evidence · agency · structure · responsibility · necessary uncertainty all intact |
| 10 | **Readability** | hard to read | ok | natural, well-shaped |

## Scoring guards (non-negotiable)

- **Dimension 9 is a gate.** If epistemic preservation = 0 (a necessary
  reasoning property was lost), the output **fails** regardless of how clean/
  short it is (this is X06/X07).
- **Concision (2) is not brevity.** It is "no wasted words *given the depth the
  user asked for*." A deep-request answer is allowed to be long and still score
  2 on concision if nothing is padding.
- **Length alone moves nothing.** Shorter does not raise 1–8; only removing
  *surplus* does. Dropping *substance* lowers 9.

## Verdict

- **Pass:** total ≥ 15/20, dimension 9 ≥ 1, no critical X-code (X06/X07/X08).
- **Fail:** dimension 9 = 0, or any critical X-code, or total < 12.
